# VALIDATION LOG

## Purpose

This document records validation results associated with the BSC-Forensics research workflow.

The purpose of the validation log is to preserve what was actually checked, what result was obtained, and what conclusion is supported by that result.

No unsupported attribution or causal conclusion is introduced by this document.

---

## 1. Validation Principles

Validation is performed before analytical interpretation where applicable.

The validation process focuses on:

- Data completeness
- Data consistency
- Reproducibility
- Canonical comparison
- Execution integrity
- State-transition consistency
- Evidence traceability

A validation result must remain distinguishable from an attribution hypothesis.
